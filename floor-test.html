HOW IT WORKS

Design it on your wall.

3D Preview: Drag anywhere outside the edge controls to orbit around the sample. Drag any of the four circular handles to change the sample size.

Floor → Wall test: Tap START AR, slowly scan the floor until tracking is ready, and a white vertical grid will appear automatically. Drag the grid sideways and forward or back until its bottom edge meets the wall. Drag its circular rotation control left or right until it lines up with the real wall. Then tap PLACE WALL to show the LUX cladding.

This experiment uses 8th Wall's tracked floor coordinate system. It does NOT detect the wall automatically. You can see and correct the alignment before placement. After placing, move sideways, closer, and look away and back to test stability. SAVE DIAGNOSTICS exports the results.
